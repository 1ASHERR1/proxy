Open the web from one address.

Type a site or a search. nebula loads the page on its side and hands it back through its own address — so your browser only ever talks to nebula.

A small, self-hosted web proxy you run yourself with node bin/unblock.js. Built for reading static and lightly-scripted sites; logins, heavily interactive sites and video are out of scope. Use it in line with the rules of whatever network you're on.
